My Team

I have been in a LEGO Robotics club for 3 years. My mom helped start it. I am in a competitive league with 7 other people, and we will compete in the FIRST LEGO League on Saturday 21st March 2026 at the American School in London.

The hardest thing in our group is learning to work together because we have to agree on different roles, share ideas, and nobody is allowed to be the boss of someone else's part of the robot.

How FIRST LEGO League Works (Summary)

  • Robot Game: Teams design and code an autonomous LEGO robot to complete missions on a themed mat within timed rounds.
  • Robot Design: Teams explain how they built and programmed their robot, including testing, improvements, and strategy.
  • Innovation Project: Teams research a real-world problem linked to the season theme and present a creative solution.
  • Core Values: Judges also look at teamwork, inclusion, discovery, impact, and how teams support one another.

So FLL is not just about points on the table. It is also about engineering, problem-solving, communication, and being a great team.
