My Reading Journey

It took me a long time to learn to read because I found the early reader books really boring. Then when I realised I could read bigger books, I skipped the easy books and went straight to bigger books.

Book Series and Authors I Love

  • Mortal Engines: I really loved this series. There were 12 books but they were all so good. None of the information was repeated and the story wasn't predictable. For example, main characters from the first 3 books eventually died, which made the story feel very real and surprising.
  • Michael Morpurgo: I love his books, especially War Horse.
  • My Side of the Mountain: The action was slow, but the descriptions were really good and helped me imagine everything.
  • David Walliams: I sometimes read his books because they make me laugh.
  • The Hobbit: This is what I'm reading right now.
  • Around the World in 80 Days: I really liked this book, and I have lots of other Jules Verne books I will read too.

Magazines, Podcasts, and Fact Books

I love reading magazines while I'm eating breakfast. My favourite is The Phoenix, which is a comic book magazine. I find it really funny. The creators and contributors clearly make comics because they love doing it and because it's fun, and that makes it extra interesting.

There is a comic book fair every year, but I missed tickets this year. I'm hoping to go next year.

I also love How It Works, a science and engineering magazine with in-depth topics. In one issue it talks about bad habits and the science of how to replace them. I really enjoy the latest tech section, and the short facts at the back are brilliant. A few favourites are:

  • "The immortal jellyfish has no maximum life span and can theoretically live forever."
  • "The world's first programmer Ada Lovelace was born in 1815, long before computers existed."
  • "France holds the record for the longest recorded traffic jam in February 1980, which was 109 miles long."

I love tiny facts like this and try to remember them.

I also just got a subscription to The Week Junior. I used to not like reading news because it felt too serious, but now that I'm older I enjoy it much more.

I sometimes listen to a podcast called The Daily, which is where I learned about Agentic AI.

I also love fact books like Guinness World Records and ask Santa for one every year. I love the weird facts. I have loads of science fact books too. My science teachers always wonder how I know so much, and a lot of it comes from these fact books.

Books are adventures in your brain 📚
