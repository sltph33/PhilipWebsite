About Me and LEGO

I've been building and collecting LEGO since I was seven years old. I am very passionate about LEGO and very protective of my sets. I also have a wall of LEGO minifigures (photo coming soon!).

In December 2025, as a gift for my 10th birthday, I went to the LEGO House in Denmark. I would have learned how LEGO bricks are designed, how creativity and engineering come together, and how LEGO history has grown from simple wooden toys into amazing modern sets.

Quick History of LEGO

  • LEGO started in Denmark in 1932, founded by Ole Kirk Christiansen.
  • The name LEGO comes from Danish words meaning "play well."
  • The modern plastic LEGO brick was introduced in 1958.
  • LEGO minifigures were introduced in 1978.
  • Today LEGO is one of the world's most popular toy brands, with themes like space, cities, trains, and giant display sets.
